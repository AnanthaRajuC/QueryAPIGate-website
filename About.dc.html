About

A data API should be as careful as the DBA who wrote the grants.

QueryAPIGate began as SQL2API: a way to turn a SQL query into an endpoint without writing the same controller for the fortieth time. It grew into a governance layer because every team that exposed data also needed to say who could ask, what they could see, and prove it afterwards. Then AI agents arrived with the same questions.

How it is built

Read-only until told otherwise

Writes are opt-in per connection or key, narrowed to specific operations, and for agents gated by a person. The default is the safe one.

Stable means stable

Features under the compatibility promise do not break in a minor release. Experimental ones are labelled in the product and the changelog, and may change.

Tested against the real thing

CI runs against real PostgreSQL, MySQL, ClickHouse, S3 and Keycloak, upgrades from the previous release, and checks the oldest supported dependencies.

Decisions written down

Architecture decision records, a threat model, a prioritised backlog and 59 how-to guides live in the repository next to the code. The components are on Built with.
