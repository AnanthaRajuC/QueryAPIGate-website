For security and compliance teams

Answer the auditor from the running system

An auditor asks who changed which grant, and how you know the record wasn't edited. A questionnaire asks where personal data goes and which cryptography protects it. The answers are the product's own behaviour, each one checkable.

✓Every administrative change in a signed chain, with a copy in your SIEM that verifies on its own. How it works →
✓Where data goes, written down and kept true by a test. history forget erases one person's runs and usage on request, audited by hash, not by name.

Compliance and audit
# sign the chain, keep a copy, stream it
QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_KEY=<a long random key>
QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_SYSLOG_URL=tls://siem.example.com:6514
QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_SYSLOG_CERT=/etc/queryapigate/client.pem
QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_SYSLOG_KEY=/etc/queryapigate/client.key

# the store, and what the SIEM received
queryapigate audit verify
# Intact: entries 1 to 5, keyed with QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_KEY.
queryapigate audit verify --file rsyslog.log
# Intact: entries 1 to 5, keyed with QUERYAPIGATE_AUDIT_KEY.

# a data subject asks to be forgotten
queryapigate history forget jwt:alice@example.com --dry-run
