Trust center

Your data stays on your infrastructure. Everything else, you can check.

QueryAPIGate is software you run, not a service we host. This page collects what a security review asks for, with each claim pointing to where you can verify it: in the code, in CI, or on your own running server.

Data flows

Rows pass through and aren't kept

Results are streamed or paged to the caller after masking, and written nowhere unless you turn on the response cache or an export. Parameter values are bound, never logged, never put in a trace; the access log leaves query strings out.

The audit chain, on your server
#1 create_connection09:02 · hmac ✓#2 create_query09:07 · hmac ✓#3 update_key09:14 · hmac ✓edited#4 create_role09:21 · hmac ✓#5 publish_query09:30 · hmac ✓
Each entry is HMAC-signed over itself and the one before, with a key kept outside the store. Edit one and every verification from it onward fails, by name.
