From caller to database, through one core

How it fits together

Every front door runs the same checks, so an AI agent's call is governed exactly like an app's. The core is the product; the front doors and drivers plug into it. Write the SQL once; QueryAPIGate publishes it as a REST endpoint, an MCP tool, a live event stream and a scheduled export, and reports every call to the systems you name.

Who callsApps, partners, AI agents, people, pipelinesBackends and dashboards, scoped partner keys, any MCP client, the Console through your SSO, cron and Kubernetes CronJobs.

Governed, not just exposed

A typed client in your team's language, generated in one command

Every server publishes a complete OpenAPI 3.0 spec, checked against the official validator in CI, with each saved query as its own typed endpoint. Point OpenAPI Generator at it and get a client in any of its roughly 50 languages: methods named after your queries, parameters typed from their rules, nothing written by hand.
